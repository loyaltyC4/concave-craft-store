# Fingerboard Lab — sister store

**[Fingerboard Lab](https://fingerboardlab.com)** is the deck-building and molds side of the same workshop:

- [Concave press molds](https://fingerboardlab.com/search/deck-building) — shallow to deep concave, with metal alignment pins and scribes
- [Maple veneer blanks](https://fingerboardlab.com/search/decks) — pressed maple, 32/34/36mm
- [Ramps & obstacles](https://fingerboardlab.com/search/ramps-obstacles) — modular wooden ramps, rails and ledges
- [Custom graphics](https://fingerboardlab.com/custom) — upload your artwork, hand-pressed onto real maple

If this repo's storefront is Concave Craft (completes and finished boards), Fingerboard Lab is where the **raw materials and pressing gear** live — veneer, molds, and everything to press your own decks.

For the sizing theory behind the widths: [Fingerboard sizing guide (29mm vs 32mm vs 34mm vs 36mm)](https://fingerboardlab.com/guides/fingerboard-sizing-guide).
